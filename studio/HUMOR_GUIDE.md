# Blobby & Co. — silent comedy guide

## Length, hook and loop (from our own analytics, Oct 2026)
ep1 (13 s, action in frame one) kept 58% of viewers and was watched 111% (replays); ep2 (17 s, slow start) kept 27%. So:
- **12–13 s total.** No end card, no question on screen, no voice line: `loop=True, voice=False` on every episode.
- **Frame one = action + sound** (shake, jump, crash, `sfx` at 0.0). Never open on characters just standing or sitting.
- **First caption 1–3 words** ("5:00 AM", "Chef mode", "Free WiFi!"). Punchline by ~8 s.
- **Loop bridge:** the last beat (1.2–1.6 s, "Tomorrow:", "Next day:", "Next night:", "One more?") puts the character back in the place and pose of beat 1, so the video flows straight into the start and the viewer does not feel it ended.
- **Human reaction sounds (`vox`): at most 2 per video**, short and non-verbal only ("Ah!", "Ooh!", "Oh no!", "Ugh!", "Mm-hm.", "Haaa...", "Hmph!"). Never words or sentences.
- One joke. Caption ≤ 2 lines, ≤ 16 chars/line. Thought bubble ≤ 4 words, max 2 bubbles. One SFX per action.
- The viewer question lives only in the description, IG/FB text and pinned comment — never highlighted in the video.
- `cover` = the second with the biggest reaction face.
- Reference batch for this format: `code/ep33_42.py`.

| Phase | Time | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| Hook | 0–1 s | Action + sound in frame one, caption 1–3 words. |
| Setup | 1–4 s | Hero does the hopeful thing. |
| Escalation | 4–7 s | 2–3 quick beats, each bigger. |
| Punchline | ~7–8 s | The twist + caption. |
| Hold | 2.5–3.5 s | Nothing happens. The hold IS the joke. |
| Loop bridge | 1.2–1.6 s | "Next day:" — back to beat 1's pose. |

## What makes it work
1. **Relatable > clever.** The viewer must think "that's me" or "that's my partner" within 2 s.
2. **The sound tells the joke.** Vine boom = the moment of truth. Sad trombone = failure. Crickets = awkward silence. Record scratch = "wait, what?". Pick one per beat, never stack three.
3. **Big faces.** Reactions are the content: wide eyes, spiral eyes, half-closed judgement (Boss, Pinky), hearts, sweat drop. The cover frame is always a reaction.
4. **One joke.** If you need a second twist, that's the next episode.
5. **The question goes in the description and pinned comment** (comments are the strongest ranking signal). Make it a choice: "A or B?", "Tag the Pinky in your life". Never on screen.
6. **Captions are read on mute by 80 % of viewers.** Every beat must make sense with no sound at all; sound makes it funnier, not understandable.

## Character logic
- **Blobby**: ambition vs reality. Starts confident, ends on the floor. Never cruel, always hopeful.
- **Pinky**: the "anything is fine" partner, the silent look, the 47 photos before posting one, the cold feet on his back.
- **Boss**: 4:59 pm meeting, "quick call", reply-all, the thumbs-up emoji. Half-closed eyes always.
- **Meow**: 3 am zoomies, knocks the cup off, sits on the laptop, stares at nothing. Meow never loses.

## Joke types that travel worldwide (no language, no culture needed)
morning alarm · diet vs fridge · gym day one · online shopping box arrives · "quick" phone check · phone battery 1 % · Monday vs Friday · boss walks past · waiting for a text reply · the cat and the full glass · partner says "we need to talk" · expectation vs reality · weekend plans vs weekend · "I'll start tomorrow" · rain + no umbrella · last slice of pizza · the gift that is socks.

## Never
- Dialogue, voice lines, words in reaction sounds, more than 2 reaction sounds, speech bubbles with full sentences.
- Politics, religion, bodies, illness, violence, real brands, song lyrics.
- Physical gag stacked on a text punchline (pick one).
- Reusing a premise with other words ("fix my laptop" after "fix my printer" is the same joke).
